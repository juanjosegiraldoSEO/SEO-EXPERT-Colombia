03   Detalle del Experto SEO

Lo que cubre un Experto SEO
de principio a fin

Transparencia total en cada entregable — desde las bases técnicas hasta contenido impulsado por IA a escala.

Auditorías y Correcciones SEO Técnico+

Auditoría Completa del Sitio

Rastreo completo con Screaming Frog, SEMrush y Ahrefs. Errores de rastreo, cadenas de redirección, contenido duplicado, páginas huérfanas — todo mapeado y priorizado por impacto.

Core Web Vitals

Mejoras de LCP, INP y CLS: optimización de imágenes, eliminación de bloqueo de renderizado, tiempos de respuesta del servidor, lazy loading y defer de JavaScript para alcanzar puntuaciones verdes.

Gestión del Presupuesto de Rastreo

Configuración de robots.txt, sitemaps XML, análisis de archivos de log y Search Console para garantizar que Googlebot priorice tus páginas más valiosas.

Infraestructura Técnica

HTTPS, hreflang, paginación, indexación mobile-first y validación de datos estructurados en todos los tipos de página. Todo para mantener la base técnica sólida.

Investigación de Keywords y SEO Semántico+

Investigación de Keywords en Profundidad

Intent-based mapping using GSC, SEMrush, and Ahrefs. Head terms, long-tail, People Also Ask, and featured snippet targets prioritized by ROI.

Agrupación Semántica

Topic cluster architecture that builds topical authority. Entity mapping and semantic relationships that signal expertise to search engines.

Análisis de Brechas Competitivas

Keywords competitors rank for that you don't — content and backlink gaps scored by business impact to maximize SEO investment efficiency.

Alineación con la Intención de Búsqueda

Page-level intent analysis (informational, transactional, commercial) ensuring content format, depth, and CTA match what users actually want.

SEO On-Page y Optimización de Contenido+

Optimización a Nivel de Página

Title tags, meta descriptions, H-tag hierarchy, URL slugs, image alt text, and content depth benchmarked against top-ranking competitors for every keyword.

Mejora de Señales E-E-A-T

Author authority markup, first-hand experience signals, expert citations, and trust indicators aligned with Google's Quality Rater Guidelines.

Optimización de Fragmentos Destacados

Structured answer blocks, definition boxes, step lists, and comparison tables designed to capture position zero and AI Overview appearances.

Integración de CRO

Content hierarchy and CTAs optimized for both search intent and conversion — ranking improvements that translate directly to leads and revenue.

Schema Markup, Enlazado Interno y SEO de Entidades+

Implementación de Schema

JSON-LD for Organization, LocalBusiness, Product, FAQ, HowTo, Article, BreadcrumbList — validated against Google's Rich Results Test.

Entidades y Knowledge Graph

Building your brand as a recognized entity in Google's Knowledge Graph. Wikidata presence, brand disambiguation, and structured entity relationships.

Estrategia de Enlazado Interno

PageRank flow optimization, pillar-cluster models, anchor text diversity, and silo structures to pass link equity to high-priority pages effectively.

Arquitectura de Información

Site structure planning for users and crawlers. Category hierarchy, faceted navigation handling, URL taxonomy, and breadcrumb implementation.

Link Building y SEO Off-Page+

Adquisición de Enlaces White-Hat

Digital PR, guest posting, broken link building, and resource page outreach. Sustainable link acquisition that builds real domain authority over time.

Análisis de Perfil de Backlinks

Full backlink audit, toxic link identification and disavow, competitor backlink gap analysis, and domain authority tracking with monthly reporting.

SEO Local, GEO y AEO+

Perfil de Google Business

Full GBP optimization: categories, service areas, Q&A, review strategy, post scheduling, and photo optimization for maximum local pack visibility.

Páginas de Ubicación Programáticas

Scalable location page creation with unique locally relevant content. NAP consistency, citation building, and geo-targeted schema at scale.

Optimización para Motores Generativos

Structuring content to appear in ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, and Gemini. Brand citation and source credibility signals for LLM visibility.

Optimización para Motores de Respuesta

Optimizing for Google AI Overviews, voice search, and conversational queries. Concise formats and structured data AI systems prefer to cite.

Analítica, Reportes y Estrategia de Crecimiento+

Configuración de Analítica y Tracking

GA4, Google Search Console, Tag Manager, conversion tracking, and custom dashboards in Power BI or Looker Studio — every decision data-informed.

Reportes Mensuales

Actionable reports covering rankings, traffic, conversions, technical health, and competitor movements with strategic recommendations for the next sprint.

Hoja de Ruta de Crecimiento Trimestral

Prioritized actions by impact and effort. OKR alignment, forecast modeling, and ROI projections connecting SEO directly to revenue targets.

Seguimiento de Rendimiento de Ads Pagados

ROAS, CPA, CTR, and conversion tracking across Google Ads and Meta Ads — integrated with organic SEO reporting for a complete growth picture.
